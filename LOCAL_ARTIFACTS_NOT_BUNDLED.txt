This archive contains the complete source code, tests, configuration, and recorded DB5 metrics.

The following files are not available to ChatGPT because they remained only on the user's local computer and therefore cannot truthfully be embedded here:

1. artifacts/cnn_gru_db5.pt - the exact trained PyTorch checkpoint from the user's 39-epoch/early-stopped run.
2. data/raw/db5/... - the downloaded NinaPro dataset files.
3. data/processed/db5_9class.npz - the user's processed dataset, unless regenerated locally.

To reproduce the trained system, either copy the existing local checkpoint/data into the documented paths or rerun the preprocessing/training pipeline.
